PATHWAY B · ACADEMIC COOPERATION

Learning with
Lighthouse Christian Academy.

Lighthouse Christian Academy (LCA) is a U.S.-based Christian distance-learning institution using the Accelerated Christian Education (A.C.E.) curriculum. Eligible students may follow an applicable LCA academic pathway while receiving locally arranged learning support through LIA.

For students officially enrolled in its programs, LCA governs academic records, graduation requirements, and diploma eligibility.

LIA language program enrollment and official LCA enrollment are distinct. Requirements and academic responsibilities may differ. Contact the LIA Office to confirm eligibility, the applicable enrollment process, and local support arrangements.
